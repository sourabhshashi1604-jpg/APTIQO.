

# APTIQO – Features Overview  
*An AI-powered career intelligence platform for everyone*

APTIQO helps individuals understand their career potential, build relevant skills, and discover the right opportunities—while enabling organizations to find and engage verified talent.  
At its core is an **AI Career Intelligence Engine** that creates a dynamic **Career Digital Twin** for every user.

***

## Authentication & Security

- **Sign Up** – Register using email.  
- **Login** – Secure access with email and password.  
- **Google Authentication** – One-click sign-in via Google.  
- **Password Recovery** – Reset password through email.  
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** – Optional 2FA for enhanced security.  
- **Session Management** – View and manage active sessions and devices.  
- **Account Controls** – Deactivate or delete account and data at any time.  

Designed for simplicity, security, and trust across all user types.

***

## User Profile

- **Personal Profile** – Name, headline, location, bio, profile photo, and social links.  
- **Education** – Institutions, degrees, durations, and key achievements.  
- **Experience** – Roles, companies, timelines, responsibilities, and impact.  
- **Skills** – Categorized skills with proficiency levels and endorsements.  
- **Certifications** – Issuers, dates, verification links, and attachments.  
- **Portfolio** – Projects, case studies, links, media, and external profiles (GitHub, Behance, etc.).  
- **Resume Upload** – Multiple resume versions (PDF/DOCX) with AI-assisted parsing.  
- **Career Preferences** – Desired roles, industries, locations, work modes, and salary expectations.  
- **Privacy Controls** – Visibility settings for profile sections and activity.  

The profile serves as the foundation for the **Career Digital Twin** and all AI-driven insights.

***

## AI Career Intelligence Engine

> Generates a dynamic **Career Digital Twin** that measures opportunity readiness, predicts career growth, identifies skill gaps, and provides personalized career guidance.

- **AI Resume Analysis** – Parses resumes, extracts key entities, and suggests improvements in content, structure, and keywords.  
- **Career DNA** – A unique, data-driven profile of strengths, interests, work style, and potential career paths.  
- **Skill Gap Analysis** – Compares current skills against target roles and markets to highlight missing or weak skills.  
- **Career Compatibility Score** – Quantifies how well a user fits specific roles, industries, and companies.  
- **Strength & Weakness Analysis** – AI-generated insights into core strengths and areas for development.  
- **Learning Recommendations** – Tailored courses, projects, and resources to close identified skill gaps.  
- **Career Growth Prediction** – Forecasts likely career paths, timelines, and earning potential based on trajectory and market trends.  
- **Opportunity Readiness Index** – A single metric summarizing how ready a user is for their next career move.  
- **What-If Scenarios** – Simulate the impact of adding skills, certifications, or changing roles on career outcomes.  

This engine powers personalized experiences for job seekers, professionals, and recruiters alike.

***

## Skill Verification

- **AI Skill Assessments** – Adaptive, role-specific quizzes and tasks evaluated by AI.  
- **Coding Assessments** – Timed coding challenges with auto-grading and detailed feedback.  
- **Domain Assessments** – Role- and industry-specific tests (engineering, data, marketing, etc.).  
- **Soft Skill Evaluation** – Scenario-based assessments for communication, teamwork, and leadership.  
- **Verified Skill Badge** – On-platform badge for skills verified through assessments, visible on profiles.  
- **Assessment History & Reports** – Track performance and improvement over time with clear analytics.  

Verified skills help users stand out regardless of background, brand of college, or employment gaps.

***

## Opportunity Explorer

- **Company Recommendations** – AI-curated list of companies aligned with user profile and goals.  
- **Internship Recommendations** – Role- and location-specific internship suggestions.  
- **Startup Opportunities** – Early-stage companies, high-growth roles, and equity-based options.  
- **Remote Jobs** – Filtered remote and hybrid opportunities across functions and seniority.  
- **Government Jobs** – Public sector roles, exams, and application tracks.  
- **International Opportunities** – Visa-friendly roles, global companies, and relocation-related information.  
- **Smart Filters** – Filter by role, skills, salary, location, work mode, experience level, and more.  

Built to support a wide range of career stages and job types, from internships to senior roles.

***

## Learning Hub

- **Personalized Learning Roadmap** – Step-by-step plan to reach target roles and skill levels.  
- **Recommended Courses** – Curated from multiple providers based on gaps and goals.  
- **Certifications** – Suggested certifications with difficulty, cost, and market value.  
- **Projects to Build** – Hands-on project ideas aligned with target roles and skills.  
- **Daily Learning Goals** – Bite-sized tasks and micro-learning prompts to maintain consistency.  
- **Progress Tracking** – Visualize completed courses, projects, and skill improvements.  
- **Learning Streaks & Gamification** – Motivational elements to encourage regular learning.  

Helps users move from “I don’t know what to learn” to a clear, actionable plan.

***

## Dashboard

- **Career Analytics** – Overview of profile strength, skill growth, and opportunity fit.  
- **Progress Tracking** – Visual progress toward goals, roadmaps, and skill targets.  
- **Career Goals** – Define, edit, and track short- and long-term career objectives.  
- **Notifications** – Application updates, new opportunities, learning reminders, and network activity.  
- **Activity Timeline** – Chronological view of profile updates, assessments, applications, and learning.  
- **Quick Actions** – Fast access to resume upload, skill assessment, job search, and AI assistant.  

A unified view of career progress and next best actions for every user.

***

## Professional Network  

APTIQO includes a **professional networking layer** that helps users connect, learn, and grow through their peers, mentors, and industry professionals.

- **People Discovery** – Find users by role, skills, company, college, location, and interests.  
- **Connect Requests** – Send and accept connection requests to build your network.  
- **Follow Model** – Option to follow influential users without requiring mutual connection.  
- **Feed & Updates** – See posts, achievements, certifications, and project updates from your network.  
- **Mentorship Matching** – AI-suggested mentors and mentees based on goals and background.  
- **Career Discussions** – Topic-based threads for advice, referrals, and industry conversations.  
- **Knowledge Sharing** – Users share resources, templates, notes, and learnings.  
- **Referrals & Recommendations** – Request and give referrals or skill endorsements.  
- **Network Insights** – Visualize your network’s industries, roles, and seniority distribution.  

This ensures that **no one navigates their career alone**, regardless of where they start.

***

## Community

- **Mentorship Programs** – Structured mentor–mentee matching and progress tracking.  
- **Career Discussions** – Forums and threads for Q&A, advice, and shared experiences.  
- **Knowledge Sharing** – Articles, guides, templates, and resources contributed by the community.  
- **Professional Networking Events** – Webinars, AMAs, and group discussions by domain and role.  
- **Clubs & Cohorts** – Interest-based groups (e.g., “Aspiring Data Engineers”, “Civil Tech Innovators”, “Career Switchers”).  

A supportive ecosystem for learning, guidance, and peer connection.

***

## AI Assistant

- **Career Chat Assistant** – Conversational AI for career questions, strategy, and planning.  
- **Resume Guidance** – Real-time suggestions to improve bullet points, structure, and keywords.  
- **Interview Preparation** – Mock interviews, question banks, and feedback by role and seniority.  
- **Career Planning** – Help define goals, map paths, and create actionable plans.  
- **Profile Optimization Tips** – AI-driven suggestions to improve profile completeness and impact.  
- **Application Support** – Guidance on tailoring resumes and profiles for specific roles.  

Acts as a 24/7 career coach that knows your profile, goals, and market context.

***

## Recruiter Platform

- **Candidate Search** – Advanced search by skills, experience, location, education, and verified badges.  
- **AI Candidate Matching** – Auto-ranked candidates for specific roles based on fit scores.  
- **Verified Profiles** – Highlight candidates with verified skills and assessments.  
- **Hiring Dashboard** – Track pipelines, stages, interviews, and offers.  
- **Talent Pools** – Save and organize candidates into custom lists.  
- **Outreach Tools** – In-app messaging and connection requests to candidates.  
- **Employer Branding** – Company pages, culture information, and featured opportunities.  

Enables organizations to discover and engage talent based on **skills and potential**, not just pedigree.

***

## Admin & Operations

- **User Management** – View, search, and manage user accounts and statuses.  
- **Company Management** – Approve and manage company profiles and recruiter access.  
- **Content Moderation** – Monitor posts, discussions, and reported content.  
- **Reports** – System-wide usage, growth, engagement, and conversion reports.  
- **Analytics** – Dashboards for KPIs such as DAU/MAU, assessments taken, applications, and hires.  
- **Feature Flags** – Control rollout of new features and experiments.  
- **Audit Logs** – Track admin actions and critical system changes.  

Ensures the platform remains secure, scalable, and well-governed.

***

## Notifications & Communication

- **In-App Notifications** – Real-time updates for matches, messages, and activity.  
- **Email Notifications** – Summaries, reminders, and important alerts.  
- **Push Notifications (Future)** – Mobile/web push for time-sensitive updates.  
- **Notification Preferences** – User-controlled channels and categories.  

Keeps all users informed without overwhelming them.

***

## Data & Privacy

- **Data Export** – Users can download their profile, assessments, and activity data.  
- **Data Deletion** – Full account and data deletion on request.  
- **Consent Management** – Clear consent flows for data usage, especially for AI features.  
- **Compliance Readiness** – Designed to align with applicable data protection regulations.  

Built to earn and maintain user trust across regions and use cases.
